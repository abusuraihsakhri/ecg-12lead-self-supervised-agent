12-Lead ECG Reference Tools

Local QT correction and frontal-plane QRS axis calculations. No ECG waveform model or diagnostic classifier runs in this page.

Not for clinical diagnosis or emergency decisions. Measurements must be checked by a qualified clinician. The simplified calculations cannot establish rhythm, acute coronary occlusion, or a treatment recommendation.

QTc calculator

Enter QT and RR, then calculate.

Bazett = QT / √RR; Fridericia = QT / RR^(1/3); Framingham = QT + 154 × (1 − RR). RR is in seconds and QT is in milliseconds.

Frontal QRS axis

Enter net QRS amplitudes, then calculate.

Axis = atan2(aVF, I), expressed in degrees. This is a simplified net-amplitude estimate, not a comprehensive vectorcardiographic assessment.
